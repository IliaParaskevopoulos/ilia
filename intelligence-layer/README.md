# Intelligence Layer

The context everything else reads from. Two parts, kept separate on purpose.

| Part | Scope | Lifespan |
|---|---|---|
| `role-context/` | This company, this quarter | Dies when you leave |
| `personal/` | What you've learned across every role | Travels with you |

**Rule:** company-specific detail (names, numbers, products) lives only in `role-context/`. Anything in `personal/` must be stripped of it, so it can move to the next role intact.

## Role context
- [goals.md](role-context/goals.md) — targets this quarter and what counts as off track
- [icp.md](role-context/icp.md) — who we sell to, their language, their objections
- [competitors.md](role-context/competitors.md) — who they are, how they position, where we win and lose
- [positioning.md](role-context/positioning.md) — our claims and the proof behind each one
- [team.md](role-context/team.md) — who reports to me, their patches, their KPIs
- [execs.md](role-context/execs.md) — who I answer to and what each of them cares about

## Personal intelligence
- [principles.md](personal/principles.md) — how I operate
- [decisions.md](personal/decisions.md) — past calls and how they turned out
