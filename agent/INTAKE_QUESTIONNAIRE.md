# Sales Plays Agent — Intake Questionnaire

Used in **Step 2** of the workflow, after ingesting existing materials
(`SYSTEM_PROMPT.md` Step 1). Only ask what wasn't already answered by the
client's existing materials. Ask conversationally, in this rough order —
push for specifics whenever an answer is vague.

## 0. Materials checklist (collect before or alongside intake)

- [ ] Website URL(s) — homepage, pricing, product pages
- [ ] Sales deck / pitch deck (latest version)
- [ ] Any existing battlecards, competitive notes, or win/loss docs
- [ ] Named competitors, ranked by how often they show up in deals
- [ ] Case studies, testimonials, published customer stats
- [ ] Pricing model (even directionally, if not public)
- [ ] CRM export or notes on recent lost deals, if available
- [ ] Brand assets: logo, primary color/hex, style guide if one exists

## 1. Company & product basics

1. In one sentence, what does the product do and for whom?
2. What problem does it solve that the buyer was previously solving badly
   (or not at all)?
3. How is it priced (model, not necessarily exact numbers)?
4. What's the company stage / size, and how mature is the sales team
   (founder-led sales vs. dedicated reps vs. a full sales org)?

## 2. Target audience / ICP

For each priority segment (aim for 2-4, not an exhaustive list):

1. Who is the buyer — title, function, seniority?
2. What company profile fits best (size, industry, tech stack, geography)?
3. What's the specific pain that makes them go looking for a solution?
4. What event or trigger tends to precede a deal starting (a recent
   incident, a new hire, a funding round, a compliance deadline)?
5. Where would you find 10 more people who look like your best customers
   right now (communities, events, job titles to search)?
6. What's a red flag that tells you a prospect is NOT a fit, even if they
   showed interest?

## 3. Competitive landscape

For each named competitor (start with the 2-4 that come up most in deals):

1. When do you lose to them? Be specific about the deal type.
2. When do you win against them? Also specific.
3. What do they do genuinely well — where would it be dishonest to claim
   we're better?
4. Where are they weak — slow implementation, pricing that scales badly,
   missing a specific feature, poor support?
5. What do you know about their pricing?
6. Has a rep ever asked a prospect a question that exposed a real
   weakness in this competitor mid-call? What was it?
7. Is there a claim reps currently make about this competitor that isn't
   fully accurate and should stop?

## 4. Objections

1. What are the 5 objections you hear most often, in the prospect's own
   words if possible?
2. For each: what do you think the prospect is *actually* worried about
   underneath the stated objection?
3. What's worked, in practice, to move past it?
4. Do you have a stat, case study, or customer quote that helps with this
   specific objection?
5. Is there an objection reps currently handle badly or inconsistently?

## 5. Proof points & market context

1. What customer results can you point to (numbers, quotes, logos you can
   name)?
2. Do you have or know of any market data, industry stats, or trend
   information relevant to why buyers need this now? (If not, say so —
   the agent will build a qualitative report from this conversation
   instead of fabricating external data.)
3. What's changed in the market in the last 12 months that makes this a
   more urgent purchase than it used to be?

## 6. Creative direction (for image/video briefs)

1. Any existing brand guidelines, color palette, tone of voice?
2. What has performed well creatively before (a post, an ad, a video) —
   what made it work?
3. Which channels matter most for this content: outbound email, LinkedIn
   organic, paid social, sales one-pagers, demo videos?
4. Any competitor or industry creative you specifically like or want to
   avoid looking like?

## 7. Wrap-up

1. Who's the internal owner who will review and approve the kit before
   it ships to reps?
2. Is there anything reps have specifically asked for that isn't covered
   above?
